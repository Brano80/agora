# Plausibility audit — 2026-10-28 (Phase 7, step A)

Every scenario family behind AGORA's published numbers, re-run for every snapshot country with the engine **unchanged** (commit after 8884d7c), and checked against the new plausibility-v2 laws in `consistency/checks.py`. The laws run in *audit* mode, so nothing shipped changes; this file reads what they flag. Reproduce: `python scripts/audit_plausibility.py`.

| Law | Fails when |
|---|---|
| `hh_net_worth_nonnegative` | a household class owes more than it owns (money + foreign assets + capital) |
| `money_split_shares_valid` | a household class holds negative money (an interest-free overdraft at the state) |
| `investment_share_bounded` | investment exceeds 40% of GDP |
| `gini_floor` | the personal Gini falls below 0.15 (lowest EU member in the data: SK 0.228) |
| `owners_mpc_in_unit_interval` | the owners' marginal propensity to consume, solved as a calibration residual, lies outside [0, 1] |

## Reading

- Every run counted above passes (or fails) the **accounting** gate exactly as before; the plausibility laws only add information.
- The dominant failure is **investment financing**: capex follows an exogenous path (+6%/yr under the AI shift), so investment outgrows 40% of GDP, and capital owners pay for it out of negative money. Under UBC that overdraft turns into insolvency because the owners' capital passes to the fund. In LV and RO it shows up even in the baseline, so it is a property of the closure, not of the AI scenario. That is the Phase 7 step B fix.
- The Gini floor mostly fails under UBC (the two-class lognormal compresses personal income below anything observed); it is an implausible-outcome flag, not an accounting defect, and is revisited after steps B-E.
- LU and SI calibrate a negative owners' MPC and IE starts with investment above 40% of GDP: those three countries' results are unusable under the current closure regardless of scenario.
- Until step B lands, no number in the rows marked implausible should be quoted; step F re-runs this audit in enforce mode on the fixed engine.
